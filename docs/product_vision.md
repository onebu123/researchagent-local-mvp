# Product Vision

ResearchAgent aims to become an auditable all-in-one research agent workspace for literature ingestion, evidence-grounded drafting, claim verification, reviewer simulation, revision planning, and exportable research audit packages.

## Users

- Students learning evidence-based research workflows.
- Researchers organizing literature, data, drafts, and revision history.
- Labs that need local, auditable project handoff artifacts.
- Course projects that need transparent provenance rather than opaque writing output.
- Manuscript draft auditors checking claims, citations, and revision plans.

## Problems It Addresses

- Literature grows faster than teams can manually organize.
- Citation metadata and source passages are difficult to verify after drafting.
- Claims can drift beyond the available evidence.
- Data analysis, figures, and manuscript text often lose their provenance links.
- Revision decisions are hard to trace across reviewer comments, patch suggestions, and human approval.

## Non-Goals

- It does not replace real experiments.
- It does not fabricate research results, references, p-values, or verified claims.
- It does not bypass human supervision, statistical review, citation checks, peer review, or ethics review.
- It does not guarantee publication, acceptance, compliance, or scientific correctness.

## Current Direction

`v2.0.1-dev` focuses on repository trust: version consistency, clean GitHub presentation, release packaging, evidence collection, CI, and a clearer command center workflow. Later releases deepen the iterative agent loop and local RAG quality without removing offline-first defaults.
